About

Sylva Ars Studio is an independent art studio focused on original paintings inspired by memory, landscape, and emotional transition.

Founded by a Cuban artist now based in the United States, the studio explores color and visual storytelling as a way of preserving emotion through art.

Rooted in the Latin words Sylva and Ars, the name suggests a forest of art shaped by emotional movement, quiet resilience, and the search for sublime beauty within both impermanence and permanence.
