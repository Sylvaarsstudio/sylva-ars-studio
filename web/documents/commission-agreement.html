Payment Terms

A non-refundable deposit is required before work begins.

The remaining balance must be paid before delivery or shipment of the artwork unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Payment plans may be accepted only through written agreement between the Artist and the Client.

Creative Process

The Client understands that the artwork is an original artistic interpretation created in the style of Sylva Ars Studio.

Minor revisions may be requested during the creation process when reasonable and mutually agreed upon.

Significant changes after approval of sketches, concepts, or progress stages may require additional fees.

Cancellation Policy

If the Client cancels the commission after work has begun, the deposit remains non-refundable.

If substantial work has already been completed, additional compensation may be required proportional to the completed work.

The Artist reserves the right to cancel the commission at any time and refund payments when necessary.

Copyright and Usage Rights

Ownership of the physical artwork transfers to the Client upon full payment.

Copyright and reproduction rights remain with Sylva Ars Studio LLC unless otherwise agreed in writing.

The Artist retains the right to display the commissioned artwork for portfolio, website, exhibition, publication, and promotional purposes.

Delivery

Delivery, shipping, insurance, framing, and packaging arrangements shall be agreed upon before final transfer.

Risk of loss or damage transfers to the Client once the artwork has been delivered or released to the shipping carrier.
